# Operational lineage graph

The lineage graph now represents the complete operational chain from a news event through observation, insight proposal, governance approval, company thesis, trade candidate, risk review, portfolio decision, fill, and realized PnL. Each node is immutable, time-aware, and restricted to finite JSON-compatible attributes. Edges can connect only adjacent valid stages, cannot travel backward in knowledge time, and cannot cross company boundaries. The prior compact event-to-fill attribution chain remains supported so historical Commit 39 data and Commit 40 PnL reports remain readable. `trace_response()` returns the focus identifier, deterministically ordered nodes, and only the edges inside that connected trace. Upstream and downstream traversal continue to support fan-in from multiple news sources and fan-out into later decisions. The expanded graph makes governance and both risk layers visible instead of collapsing them into one generic decision node. PnL attribution accepts either a complete legacy trace or the new complete operational trace and still rejects incomplete provenance. The response is backend-ready structured data for Commit 47's explorer and future visualization work.
