# Lookahead-Bias Test Suite (Commit 36)

This suite protects historical experiments from using information that was unavailable at the simulated decision time. It treats `knowledge_time` as the visibility boundary instead of trusting an article publication date, accounting period, or other event timestamp. A deliberately delayed news item proves that an older publication cannot appear before ingestion. An earnings record proves that a completed fiscal period does not reveal results before their release becomes known. Original and subsequently revised fundamentals prove that the revision cannot replace or influence the value visible in an earlier run. Market fixtures prove that future prices remain absent from as-of queries until their recorded market knowledge time. Promoted insights prove that future shared-memory entries cannot reach trade construction and that expired entries cannot reappear. Every boundary is also tested at the exact knowledge timestamp to ensure information becomes visible when, and only when, it is knowable. These tests fail loudly if a storage adapter or context gateway later filters by the wrong timestamp, providing a permanent guard against inflated backtest performance.
